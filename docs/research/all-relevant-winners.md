## How to use this evidence

- ClickHouse examples are useful when an analytical answer changes an agent/product action; they do not establish a required engine feature or a win rate.
- Guild examples support visible hosted execution and an understandable job; some historical awardees had weak or unverified integration, which is not evidence to copy that weakness.
- Semgrep's older Best Use awards support memorable presentation, not a prediction of this event's vulnerability-finding prize.
- The selected archive lacks judge scorecards, current entrant counts and an unbiased denominator. Reasons for winning remain inference.
