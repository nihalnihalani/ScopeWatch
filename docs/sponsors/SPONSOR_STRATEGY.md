| Track | Evidence-supported strong claim | Honest weaker claim |
|---|---|---|
| ClickHouse | “This actual query selected the candidate from qualified native evidence and retained the historical crossing; this separate replay measurement reports p50/p95 with actual n for the named workload.” | “We stored/queried captured events; scale and full decision semantics remain unmeasured/unverified.” |

| Semgrep | “This actual detector found this genuine issue in our ordinarily generated code, with original provenance, owned consequence and corrected/rescanned result.” | “We scanned for quality; no authentic interesting eligible finding was obtained.” |
| Pi | “This demonstrated case gives Maya a useful narrower decision and verifies approved work survives.” | “This is a proposed application of existing controls; innovation and effect are not yet demonstrated.” |

Avoid “world first,” “all agents contained,” “no false positives,” “zero vulnerabilities,” “30 customer records stolen,” “millions of real attacks,” and “subsecond containment” from SQL timing. A permission decision is not a completed read, a scan is not a complete security proof, and a native policy application is not an observed effect until verified.
