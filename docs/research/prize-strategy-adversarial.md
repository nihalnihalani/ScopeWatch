# Independent prize-strategy review

Reviewed 9 October 2026. Scope: product planning and prize arithmetic against [RECOMMENDATION.md](archive/RECOMMENDATION.md), using its supplied current prize packet. No new source verification, technical security analysis or implementation. Akash excluded.

**Recommendation: keep the evidence/investigation product direction, but make the sponsor order conditional. Do not commit to the specific case before a genuine eligible finding exists.** The brief's opening “Build BoundaryProof” is stronger than the present evidence supports. It has a proposed story, no implemented product and no authentic finding. Its later fallback gates are good; those conditions should lead the executive decision.

## Arithmetic and value

| Sponsor | Known cash/gift-card face-value pool | One team's top award | Lower awards |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| ClickHouse | $1,750 | $1,000 gift card | $500 gift card; $250 cash/Amazon gift card |
| Guild | $2,000 | $1,000, payment form unconfirmed | Two $500 runners-up |
| Semgrep | $1,500 | $1,000 gift card | $500 gift card |
| Total | **$5,250** | **$3,000 only if top awards stack** | A team cannot collect the whole pool |

The existing sums are correct. The $3,000 figure is a ceiling, with no established probability or expected payout. A second-place award in each of these three tracks would total $1,500 if stacking allows it. Guild's two runner-up slots are separate teams' opportunities, not two $500 awards available to the same entrant.

All three top awards tie at $1,000. An additional eligible sponsor offers another possible award when stacking is allowed; adding its logo alone does not raise monetary value. If stacking is forbidden, three integrations do not raise the known maximum above $1,000, before any separately stackable Pi award. Concentrate effort on the strongest eligible entry.

Keep gift cards at face value for the user's stated cash/gift-card objective, while naming their form. Do not describe the total as $3,000 cash. Guild's payout method, card restrictions and any choice between card types need confirmation. Credits and Pi hardware stay outside this subtotal. Pi's unknown gift card stays unpriced. If optimizing each teammate's share, a four-person equal split of a $3,000 stacked top-award outcome is $750 per person; this is conditional arithmetic, not a forecast. Team payout and individual payout are different objectives.

## Opportunity cost and product commitment

The three-sponsor story has coherent roles, but it still needs three distinct proof obligations and one comprehensible demonstration. In a five-hour event, a 40-minute search consumes 13.3% of the entire window. The current schedule reserves 2:20–4:30 for validation, polish, recording and submission, leaving only 170 minutes beforehand, including that search and setup. Four parallel lanes help only if the team can integrate them promptly.

Semgrep currently has the most uncertain entry gate: no authentic finding exists, and custom-rule attribution is unresolved. Its $1,000 ceiling does not compensate automatically for open-ended discovery. Treat the finding award as an option that becomes a priority after evidence appears. A compelling existing eligible finding would reverse that assessment.

Keep the product contract at “one case, one useful investigation decision, one inspectable outcome.” Make the named case and opening slogan provisional. A case chosen to fit a polished pitch can waste the event window or leave the team presenting a weaker eligibility claim. A different authentic case that is clear to judges is preferable to defending the original idea at all costs.

More prize slots do not establish better odds without entrant counts and judging evidence. Guild's two $500 runner-up awards compare favorably with ClickHouse's $500/$250 lower awards as a payout structure; this is a tie-breaker after feasibility and fit, not a probability estimate. Historical selected winners also cannot establish a win rate or prove that three sponsors outperform two.

## Conditional priority order and evidence gates

| Evidence at the event | Priority change |
|---|---|
| Stacking confirmed; no qualifying finding; Guild and ClickHouse accounts work | Build the stronger of Guild/ClickHouse first, then the companion. Keep Semgrep discovery time capped and Pi in the pitch. |
| A genuine, interesting finding exists and the sponsor confirms the actual discovery path qualifies | Move Semgrep to the lead; add the companion sponsor that most improves that case and already works. |
| Custom-rule route is rejected or remains unresolved at the cutoff | Remove Semgrep prize eligibility from the plan. Its tool use may remain in the story. |
| Guild hosted run and useful outcome are demonstrable; ClickHouse has no meaningful analytics question | Guild first. Do not spend the remaining window manufacturing a second use case. |
| ClickHouse answers a meaningful decision question; Guild setup or hosted outcome remains blocked | ClickHouse first. Cut Guild from the required demo. |
| Stacking prohibited | Choose one strongest eligible $1,000 track. Extra sponsors must justify their product contribution within the time budget. |
| Pi gift-card amount or innovation criteria prove materially attractive | Reassess pitch effort and priority using those confirmed terms; still avoid an unnecessary integration. |
| Team has fewer than four people, or the first complete workflow slips | Reduce to one primary and one working companion sponsor. Protect recording and submission time. |

At kickoff, obtain a written or saved answer on stacking, sponsor opt-in, the Semgrep discovery route, payout form and submission requirements. Give setup/discovery 20–30 minutes before choosing the lead track; require a complete core story by roughly 1:30 PM. Add another sponsor only when its evidence is already tangible and it improves that story. Freeze the demo path by about 2:30 PM, retain the existing recording/upload margin and submit well before 4:30 PM.

**The ranking should follow evidence, not the theoretical $3,000 headline.** Today the defensible default is a conditional Guild/ClickHouse core, an opportunistic Semgrep finding entry and Pi as an unpriced pitch opportunity. The exact order between Guild and ClickHouse cannot be settled without team skills, event access and a working proof of each sponsor's role.
