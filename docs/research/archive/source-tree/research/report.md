## How to read the evidence

Verified award means a structured event award badge or an explicit sponsor announcement. A sponsor list, technology tag or creator claim alone does not qualify. Rank unconfirmed means category membership is known but placement is not independently published. Creator-reported ranks remain labeled throughout.

Repository verified means the project identity follows a submission or corroborated creator link. Source inspected means selected files were read without installing, running or deploying the project. Current source may differ from the judged version. Linked videos were not independently played, and hosted demos were not exercised unless specifically stated.

Why it may have won is analysis. No individual judge scorecards were found. Sponsor retrospectives can explain a project's value without proving which factor caused its award. Medical, legal, security and performance claims remain prototype claims unless supported separately.

## Defensive patterns supported by the precedents

- Evidence changes a decision. RedBot, Darwin and policyDiff connect stored findings or deterministic checks to an agent's next action. Their value is clearer than a sponsor logo or an isolated API call.
- Authority has visible limits. Vigil, Phalanx and DailyGate show denial, approval or permission ceilings. Their code also exposes fallback behavior, so a design review should ask what happens when the governing service fails.
- Repeated checks preserve history. IncidentLogica and SynapseCRO describe durable incident or audit history. Their missing source repositories make them design references rather than implementation examples.
- Integration claims need local proof. Sentinel's host is configurable; RxScout's inspected paths do not corroborate Guild; WildFire describes future integration; zeroCMO differs from its submission. Award recognition is not an implementation audit.
- Useful prototypes remain prototypes. Distinguish mocked, seeded and live paths; check licenses before reuse; do not reuse exposed credentials or test third-party systems. No project here was certified secure or production-ready.
